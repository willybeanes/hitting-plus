Grades are built from Statcast pitch tracking, 2024 to 2026, and calibrated against xwOBA. Decision+ scores swing and take choices against the league optimum for that pitch and count. Swing+ measures contact timing, specifically how far out front a hitter is on soft stuff relative to fastballs. Contact+ is whiff avoidance. Power+ combines location-adjusted bat speed with location-adjusted lift.

Hitting+ is refit on all four components rather than averaged, because the components correlate and averaging counts the same tendency more than once. It grades the swing, so it cannot see baserunning: hitters who stretch singles into doubles will beat their grade by design.
